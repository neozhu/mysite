## Still Managing Fabric Quotations with Excel, Email and Manual Engineering Handoffs?

For a paper machine clothing manufacturer, a quotation depends on the customer's machine, operating position, fabric design and commercial rules. When these details live in separate files and conversations, every handoff adds work.

- Product and machine information is scattered across Excel files.
- Sales relies on experienced application engineers to select the correct fabric.
- Technical recommendations move through email and informal conversations.
- Pricing rules and historical prices are difficult to apply consistently.
- Discount requests have no clear approval trail.
- Revised quotations are difficult to compare and trace.
- Critical application knowledge stays with a few experienced employees.

Fabric Quotation brings that information and those decisions into a shared process for Sales, Application Engineering and commercial approvers.

## One Workflow from Customer RFQ to Final Quotation

Generic CPQ follows **Configure → Price → Quote**. Fabric Quotation is a vertical CPQ and application engineering platform built around the complete PMC sales process.

<ol class="fq-workflow" aria-label="PMC quotation workflow">
  <li><strong>1. Customer RFQ</strong>Start from the customer's requirements.</li>
  <li><strong>2. Mill / Machine / Position</strong>Establish the operating context.</li>
  <li><strong>3. Fabric Selection</strong>Select products for the relevant positions.</li>
  <li><strong>4. Application Engineering</strong>Review each quotation line.</li>
  <li><strong>5. Design Recommendation</strong>Complete the technical configuration.</li>
  <li><strong>6. Standard Pricing</strong>Apply pricing and preserve a snapshot.</li>
  <li><strong>7. Discount Approval</strong>Review the requested commercial terms.</li>
  <li><strong>8. Quotation</strong>Prepare a version with traceable decisions.</li>
</ol>

Sales, engineering and pricing decisions stay connected to the same quotation line, so the next person can continue with the information already captured.

## Built Around the Way PMC Business Actually Works

Fabric quotation goes beyond SKU selection. Each quotation starts within the customer's real operating context:

**Customer → Mill → Paper Machine → Position → Fabric**

Browse the customer's mills and machines, locate the position and select the associated fabric. Product-line grouping helps teams navigate forming, press and dryer applications without losing the machine context.

Each product also has a unique MMP number for identification and lookup.

## Application Engineering Built into the Quotation Process

Move technical review into the quotation itself. Sales can submit quotation lines to Application Engineering instead of repeatedly exchanging parameters through Excel, email or Teams.

Application engineers can:

- Review machine and position information.
- Select or recommend the fabric design.
- Complete product-specific technical parameters.
- Record engineering recommendations.
- Return the completed design to Sales for pricing and quotation preparation.
- Preserve design decisions with the quotation's history.

This engineering handoff is a core difference from generic CPQ: the technical recommendation remains attached to the product and quotation it supports.

## Flexible Technical Parameters for Different Fabric Families

Different product lines need different engineering information. Administrators can configure technical fields, sections, validation rules and option lists for:

- Forming Fabrics
- Press Felts
- Dryer Fabrics
- Shoe Press Sleeves
- Top Belts
- Additional product families as requirements evolve

Add or adjust technical parameters without redesigning the application's core structure. When a field is retired, its historical quotation values remain available.

## Controlled Pricing and Discount Management

**Sales can work efficiently without losing pricing control.** Connect the recommended design to standard pricing, then keep the requested discount and approved discount separate.

- Maintain standard pricing by manufacturing site and design.
- Use a shared quotation currency across all lines.
- Preserve the **Standard Unit Price snapshot** for each quotation version.
- Record requested discounts and the reasons behind them.
- Record approved discounts and approval decisions.
- Review approval history and compare historical prices across quotation versions.

Changes to current pricing do not overwrite the price snapshots behind earlier quotations.

## Every Quotation Version Remains Traceable

When technical configuration, pricing or commercial conditions change, create a new quotation version while preserving previous decisions.

For example, **Quote 2026-001** could progress through:

- **V1 — Initial proposal:** selected fabrics and the first technical recommendation.
- **V2 — Revised design:** updated parameters and the corresponding pricing.
- **V3 — Approved discount:** the agreed commercial proposal.

Review earlier versions to understand what changed. Workflow and audit history help teams trace who performed actions, approval decisions, historical pricing and engineering recommendations.

The application uses .NET 10, Blazor Server, MudBlazor and Entity Framework Core. Clean Architecture separates domain, application, infrastructure and UI responsibilities, with Mediator, FluentValidation and Mapster supporting application use cases.
